Master Circular -- Cash Withdrawal Limits (Revised)

In supersession of circular no. RBI-DCC-2017-01 dated 30 January 2017, the Reserve Bank of India hereby revises the cash withdrawal limits as follows:

1. Revised Cash Withdrawal Limits

With effect from the date of this circular, the weekly limit on cash withdrawals from bank accounts through any mode shall be revised to Rs. 24,000 (Rupees Twenty-Four Thousand Only) per week per account holder.

2. Prior Circular Superseded

Circular RBI-DCC-2017-01 dated 30 January 2017 stands superseded by this circular to the extent specified above.

3. Applicability

These revised limits apply with immediate effect to all Scheduled Commercial Banks and their customers.
