Master Circular -- Cash Withdrawal Limits

The Reserve Bank of India issues this circular to all Scheduled Commercial Banks.

1. Cash Withdrawal Limits from Bank Accounts

In order to manage liquidity following the currency exchange notification dated 08 November 2016, the following limits on cash withdrawals are imposed:

The weekly limit on cash withdrawals from bank accounts (savings, current, and cash credit) through ATMs, over-the-counter, or any other mode shall not exceed Rs. 10,000 (Rupees Ten Thousand Only) per week per account holder.

2. Applicability

These limits apply with immediate effect to all individuals, firms, and companies.

3. Review

The limits specified in this circular will be reviewed periodically.
