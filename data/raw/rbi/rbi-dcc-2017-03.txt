Master Circular -- Removal of Cash Withdrawal Limits

In supersession of circular no. RBI-DCC-2017-02 dated 20 February 2017, the Reserve Bank of India hereby removes restrictions on cash withdrawals from savings bank accounts as follows:

1. Revised Cash Withdrawal Limits

The weekly limit on cash withdrawals from savings bank accounts is revised to Rs. 50,000 (Rupees Fifty Thousand Only) per week with effect from 13 March 2017. Withdrawals from current and cash credit accounts will be subject to the account's normal sanctioned limits.

2. Prior Circular Superseded

Circular RBI-DCC-2017-02 dated 20 February 2017 stands superseded by this circular.

3. Applicability

This circular is applicable to all Scheduled Commercial Banks with immediate effect. Banks should communicate these revised limits to their customers through all available channels.
