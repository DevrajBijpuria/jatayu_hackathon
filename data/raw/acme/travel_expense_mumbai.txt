Travel Expense Limits — Mumbai Region

Employees traveling to or within Mumbai for business purposes may claim travel expenses up to $4,000 per trip.

Claims must be submitted within 30 days of trip completion with original receipts.

Hotel accommodation must not exceed $200 per night unless pre-approved by the CFO.
