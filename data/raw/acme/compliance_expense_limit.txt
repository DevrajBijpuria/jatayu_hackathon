Compliance Department Expense Controls

Employees in the Compliance Department are subject to a stricter expense approval limit of $2,000 per transaction, regardless of the standard policy limits.

All Compliance Department expense claims above $500 require dual approval: direct manager and the Chief Compliance Officer.

This policy applies in addition to any general expense policy and takes precedence where amounts differ.
