Frequently Asked Questions — Expenses and Approvals

Q1: How do I submit an expense report?
A1: Submit expense reports through the company's expense management system within 30 days of incurring the expense. Attach all receipts.

Q2: What is the manager approval limit for expenses?
A2: Managers can approve expenses up to $5,000 per transaction.

Q3: What expenses are not reimbursable?
A3: Personal entertainment, fines and penalties, and political contributions are not reimbursable.

Q4: Who approves expenses above the manager limit?
A4: Expenses above the manager limit require Director or CFO approval depending on the amount.

Q5: How long does reimbursement take?
A5: Reimbursement is typically processed within 10 business days of approval.
