Annual Paid Leave Entitlement

All full-time employees of Acme Corporation are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Leave must be pre-approved by the employee's direct manager at least 5 business days in advance.

Unused leave up to a maximum of 5 days may be carried forward to the following year.

Leave encashment is not permitted except upon separation from employment.
