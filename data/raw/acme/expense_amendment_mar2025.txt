ACME CORPORATION — EXPENSE POLICY AMENDMENT
Document ID: ACME-EXP-AMD1
Issuer: Finance Department
Issued Date: 2025-03-01
Document Type: amendment

Subject: Amendment to Expense Reimbursement Policy — Manager Approval Limit

This amendment revises section 4.2 of Expense Reimbursement Policy (ACME-EXP-V1) dated 2023-01-15.

1. Amendment

In supersession of section 4.2 of ACME-EXP-V1, the manager approval limit is revised as follows:

Revised Section 4.2 — Manager Approval
Managers may approve expenses up to $3,000 per transaction for their direct reports, effective from 2025-03-01.

All other sections of the Expense Reimbursement Policy remain unchanged.

2. Reason for Amendment
The reduction in the manager approval limit is in response to updated internal audit recommendations to strengthen financial controls.
