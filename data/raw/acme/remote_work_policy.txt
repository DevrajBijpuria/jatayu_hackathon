Remote Work Guidelines

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval and business needs.

Remote work days must be agreed upon in advance with the team.

Employees working remotely are expected to be available during core business hours (10am to 4pm).

All remote work arrangements must be reviewed and renewed every 6 months.
