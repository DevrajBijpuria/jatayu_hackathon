ACME CORPORATION — EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY (REVISED)
Document ID: ACME-EXP-V2
Issuer: Finance Department
Issued Date: 2025-07-01
Document Type: policy

This policy supersedes ACME-EXP-V1 in its entirety.

1. Purpose
This revised policy governs the reimbursement of business expenses incurred by employees of Acme Corporation.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Acme Corporation.

3. General Principles
Employees are expected to exercise reasonable judgment and spend company funds responsibly.

4. Approval Limits

4.1 Employee Self-Approval
Employees may self-approve and submit for reimbursement any individual expense up to $750.

4.2 Manager Approval
Managers may approve expenses up to $3,000 per transaction for their direct reports.

4.3 Director Approval
Directors may approve expenses up to $20,000 per transaction.

4.4 CFO Approval
The CFO may approve any expense above $20,000.

5. Reimbursement Process
Employees must submit expense reports within 30 days of incurring the expense.
All receipts must be attached to the expense report.
Expense reports must be submitted through the company's expense management system.

6. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
Personal entertainment, fines and penalties, and political contributions are not reimbursable.
