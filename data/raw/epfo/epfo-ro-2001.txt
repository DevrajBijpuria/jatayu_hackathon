Sub: Wage Ceiling for Provident Fund Contributions

The Employees Provident Fund Organisation hereby notifies all employers covered under the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952:

1. Wage Ceiling for PF Contributions

The monthly wage ceiling for the purpose of determining provident fund contributions under the EPF Scheme, 1952 is set at Rs. 6,500 (Rupees Six Thousand Five Hundred Only) per month.

2. Contribution Rates

Employee contribution: 12% of monthly wages up to Rs. 6,500.
Employer contribution: 12% of monthly wages up to Rs. 6,500 (of which 8.33% goes to Employees Pension Scheme and the balance to EPF).

3. Voluntary Higher Contribution

Employees may voluntarily contribute on wages above the ceiling, subject to mutual agreement with the employer.
