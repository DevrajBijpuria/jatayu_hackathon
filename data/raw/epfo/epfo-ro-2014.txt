Sub: Revision of Wage Ceiling for Provident Fund Contributions

In supersession of circular no. EPFO-RO-2001 dated 01 June 2001, the Employees Provident Fund Organisation notifies the revised wage ceiling effective from 01 September 2014:

1. Revised Wage Ceiling

The monthly wage ceiling for provident fund contributions under the EPF Scheme, 1952 is revised to Rs. 15,000 (Rupees Fifteen Thousand Only) per month, superseding the previous limit of Rs. 6,500 per month.

2. Revised Contribution Basis

Employee contribution: 12% of monthly wages up to Rs. 15,000.
Employer contribution: 12% of monthly wages up to Rs. 15,000.

3. Prior Circular Superseded

Circular EPFO-RO-2001 dated 01 June 2001 is superseded by this circular with effect from 01 September 2014.

4. Applicability

All employers and employees covered under the EPF & MP Act, 1952 are required to comply with these revised wage ceiling norms.
